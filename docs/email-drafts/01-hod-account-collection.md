Dear Colleagues,

We are standardizing access to the Lake Cement MIS dashboard so that every login belongs to a named person with a company email address, instead of the shared departmental logins we have been using since the system went up.

**Why we are doing this**

Right now the MIS has 43 departmental accounts, but they are role slots — `production_senior`, `stores_officer` and so on — not people. Three consequences, all of which we need to close before the system is formally handed over:

- When a reading is entered or a report is approved, the audit trail records the slot, not the person. We cannot tell who did what.
- 37 of the 43 accounts are still on the original setup password that was issued when the system was built. Those need to be replaced with passwords each user sets themselves.
- We hold no email address for any user, so we cannot send a password reset, we cannot verify an account belongs to who it claims, and the daily KPI digest and downtime alerts have nowhere to go.

Moving to one person, one company email, one login fixes all three at once.

**What we need from you**

Please complete the attached sheet for your department and return it **by Friday 05 September 2026**. One row per person who needs MIS access. If you prefer, reply to this mail with the same details in a table — the columns are:

| # | Full name | Company email | Mobile | Staff no. | Job title | Access level | Enters data or views only? | Replaces which current login |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Access levels** — please assign one per person:

- **L1 — HOD.** Full department view, enters and edits data, and is the only level that can sign off a departmental report.
- **L2 — Senior / Supervisor.** Enters and edits data, reviews the department's reports, cannot give final sign-off.
- **L3 — Officer / Operator.** Enters daily readings on their own forms and views their department's dashboards.

**Three things to note**

1. **Company mailboxes only.** Please do not submit personal Gmail or Yahoo addresses — the account is the company's, not the individual's. If someone on your list has no company mailbox yet, still include them and write "no mailbox" in the email column; IT will create one.
2. **Tell us who to remove.** If a person on your current list has left, changed department, or no longer needs the dashboard, say so. We will close their access rather than carry it forward.
3. **Shift and weighbridge staff.** If you have operators who need to enter readings but have no mailbox and are unlikely to get one, flag them separately and we will agree a supervised arrangement for those roles.

**What happens after you reply**

- We add the email field to the system and load your list.
- Each person receives a verification mail at the address you gave us, and sets their own password on first login. Nobody else, IT included, will know that password.
- The old shared departmental logins are switched off.
- Once every department has replied, the daily KPI digest and plant alert mails start going out to the addresses on these lists.

Departments that have not replied by 05 September will keep working on the existing shared logins for now, but those accounts will not be carried into the final handover.

Please send any questions to me directly.
