# User Guide — Nyati Cement Reporting System

**For:** everyone who enters daily reports or KPI figures
**Version:** 1.0 · 2026-09-30

This guide is written for plant staff, not for computer specialists. If
anything here does not match what you see on your screen, the screen is
right and this guide is out of date — please tell us.

---

## 1. What this system is

It is the place where Nyati Cement's daily plant reports are recorded. The
reports you used to fill in on paper or in Excel are now entered here once,
and the system does the rest: it calculates the KPIs, builds the management
reports, and keeps the history.

**Why it matters to you:** when you enter your figures here, you do not
have to re-type them into anything else. Your department head sees them,
management sees them, and the monthly reports build themselves.

---

## 2. Logging in

1. Open your browser and go to the system address:
   **https://smr.lakecement.co.tz**
2. Enter your username and password.
3. Press **Log in**.

### The first time you log in

You will be asked to set your own password before you can do anything else.
This is deliberate — nobody else should know your password, including IT and
including whoever set up your account.

Choose something you will remember. You will use this system every day.

### If you cannot log in

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Invalid credentials" | Check caps lock. The password is case-sensitive |
| Too many wrong attempts | Your account locks for a short period. Wait a few minutes and try again — this protects your account |
| "Too many attempts from this device" | Same thing. Wait a few minutes |
| You have forgotten your password | Use **Forgot password** on the login page. ⚠️ *Email is not yet working — see §9. For now, contact your administrator instead* |
| Your account is disabled | Contact your administrator |

### Staying logged in

You can work all shift without being logged out. The system quietly keeps
your session alive while you are using it, so you will not lose your work
part-way through entering a report.

If you finish for the day, use **Logout** in the menu. This properly ends
your session.

The menu on the left is the same on every page.

## 4. Filling in a daily report

### Step by step

1. Go to **Daily Reports** and choose your department's report.
2. Check the **date** at the top. This is the date the figures are *for* —
   not today's date, if you are entering yesterday's shift.
3. Enter your figures.
4. Press **Save Draft** if you are not finished, or **Submit** when you are.

### ⚠️ The most important thing in this guide

**Save Draft and Submit are not the same thing.**

| | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save Draft** | Keeps your work so you can come back to it. **Nobody else sees the figures. They do not go into any report or KPI.** |
| **Submit** | Files the report officially. The figures go to your department head and into the plant's KPIs |

A report left as a draft is **not reported**. If you fill in a report,
save it as a draft, and go home, as far as the plant's records are
concerned **that day was never reported.**

So: draft while you work, and **always Submit when you are done.**

### Coming back to a draft

Open the same report and the same date. Everything you typed will be there,
including any notes, table rows and names. If something is missing, that is
a fault — report it.

### What happens after you Submit

| Your level | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Officer or Senior** | The report goes to your department head as **Pending approval**. It enters the plant's KPIs only once they approve it |
| **Department Head** | Your submission is approved as you file it — you are signing your own figures |

You cannot approve your own report if you are an officer or senior. That is
deliberate: the person who reports a figure and the person who signs it off
are never the same person.

## 5. Checking the status of your reports

Each report is in one of four states:

| Status | Meaning | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved but not filed. **Not counted anywhere** | Finish it and Submit |
| **Pending approval** | Filed, waiting for your department head | Nothing — wait |
| **Approved** | Signed off and counted in the plant's KPIs | Nothing |
| **Rejected** | Your department head sent it back | Read the reason, correct it, submit again |

Check the reports list to see where each of yours stands. If you see one of
yours still sitting as a **Draft** from last week, that day is missing from
the plant's records.

## 6. Correcting a mistake

### Before it is approved

Just open the report and fix it, then submit again.

### After it is approved — use a correction request

Once a report is approved it is locked. If you try to change it you will see
a message saying the report is locked. **This is not a fault.** Approved
figures have already gone into the plant's reports, so they cannot be
changed quietly behind everyone's back.

To correct an approved report:

1. Open the report.
2. Choose **Request a correction**.
3. Say what is wrong and why it needs changing.
4. Your department head reviews the request.
5. If they agree, the report unlocks **for a limited time**.
6. Make your correction and save it **within that time**. If the time runs
   out, the figures go back to what they were and you have to ask again.

**You never need to ask a developer to change a figure.** If somebody tells
you that is the only way, they are wrong — use this process, so the change
is recorded properly with a reason.

## 7. Monthly KPI figures

*Department heads only. Officers and seniors do not enter monthly KPIs.*

1. Go to **KPI** and choose your department, year and month.
2. Enter the target and actual figures.
3. When the month is complete and correct, press **Submit**.

**Once you submit the month, it locks.** You cannot change it afterwards.
Only an administrator or the MD can reopen it, and they must type a written
reason which is permanently recorded.

So check the figures before you submit the month. An empty month cannot be
submitted at all.

---

## 8. Looking at the numbers

**Analytics** shows charts and trends for the data you are allowed to see.
Pick a date range and the charts update.

### Two things to understand about the numbers

**"No data" is not zero.** If a KPI says *no data*, nobody filed the report
it needs. If it said "0" instead, that would look like the plant used no
power or had no downtime — which would be wrong. The system deliberately
refuses to show a zero it cannot prove.

**Some KPIs are marked "provisional".** That means there is a known caveat
about how the figure is calculated, written out next to it. **Read that note
before quoting the number in a meeting.**

## 10. Common problems

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Report is locked" | The report is already approved | Use **Request a correction** (§6) |
| "Password change required" | First login, or your password was reset | Set a new password |
| "Session expired" | You were away a long time | Log in again |
| "Access denied" | Your role does not cover this | Normal — not a fault. Contact your head if you believe you need it |
| The KPI month will not save | The month is submitted and locked | Ask an administrator to reopen it, with a reason |
| Figures you entered are not in the KPIs | Almost always: the report is still a **Draft**, or still **Pending approval** | Submit it, or ask your head to approve it |
| A number looks wrong | **Report it — do not work around it** | See §11. Wrong numbers are the most serious kind of problem |

---

## 11. Getting help

**If a number looks wrong, report it immediately.** Do not assume it will
correct itself and do not quietly use your own figure instead. A wrong
number on a management dashboard gets used to make decisions.

When you report a problem, include:

1. Your username and role
2. The page you were on
3. The report type and the **date** of the report
4. What you expected, and what you actually saw
5. The exact wording of any error message

That information usually turns a day of investigation into ten minutes.
