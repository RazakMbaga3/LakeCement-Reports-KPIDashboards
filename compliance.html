      Data to complete
      What stops this register from being the single source of truth. Click a box to see those records.

      Every reminder the system has planned, sent or held back, and why. A reminder goes to the responsible person when the first reminder day is reached, adds the supervisor at the second, and the central Head list at the third; once overdue all three are reminded weekly. Each one is sent once. Records still waiting to be verified are held — they go out the day the department confirms the record.
