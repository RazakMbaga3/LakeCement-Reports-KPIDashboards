Method Notes & Differences vs Excel Workbook

  • Total Material Handling (the two headline cards + chart) counts each ticket once. Stock-Yard reissues (material already counted once when it moved mine→stock) are excluded from TMH when that material also has a direct mine-sourced route — otherwise a tonne mined into stock and later reclaimed to the crusher was counted twice. Limestone SPG and Gypsum have no mine-sourced route at all, so their Stock Yard movement is kept as their only handling record. This is a one-time methodology fix — if this month's TMH reads lower than a month reported before this change, that's the double-count coming out, not less material moved. The detailed Movement Matrix below still shows every route, including the reissues, for audit.
  • Closing stock = opening + all receipts into the stock yard (South, North) − issues out of stock. The Excel MIS omitted North receipts from closing while the "Supply to date" ledger included them; this report follows the ledger, so closing always equals the next day's opening.
  • Deliver to Plant counts every flow reaching the pile (South + North + Stock Yard, through-crusher and bypass). The Excel column omitted North mine direct deliveries.
  • LG1 — Other Usages uses the LG1 stock-to-other-usage tickets plus the manual LCL/Digalla/On Africa columns. OB — Other Usages uses OB stock-to-other-usage tickets. (The Excel cells referenced neighbouring columns by off-by-one lookup.)
  • Gypsum — Deliver to Plant uses the Gypsum crusher + Direct (LCL) columns (the Excel cell referenced the MG-HG columns).
  • MTD accumulates from the 1st of the month to the selected date. YTD accumulates over the fiscal year starting 1 April (matching the workbook's April→March layout). Months without imported tickets contribute zero — import each month's file to keep YTD complete.
  • Via Crusher & Lime Consumption additionally include MG-LG stock-to-crusher, which the Excel formula skipped.
