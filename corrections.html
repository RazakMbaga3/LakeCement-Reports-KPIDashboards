Daily Reports / Corrections

Correct a submitted report

Approved reports are locked, so the figures management reads cannot change without anyone knowing. To fix a mistake, ask for a correction here: an approver reopens the report for you, you fix it, and it is signed off again. Every step is recorded, and the old figures are kept.

Approvals
  1. 1
    Pick the report and the day
    Choose one day or several, and say what is wrong. Days whose figures are all zero or blank are flagged for you.
  2. 2
    An approver reopens it
    Your HOD (or an administrator / the MD) sees the request on their Approvals page and opens a correction window — 24 hours by default.
  3. 3
    Fix it and submit again
    Press Fix it now, correct the figures and submit. The report goes back for sign-off. If the window closes unused, nothing changes.
